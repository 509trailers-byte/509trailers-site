Know Before You Rent

Rental Policies

Clear and straightforward. No surprises — just everything you need to know before, during, and after your rental.

Last updated June 2026  ·  Questions? (360) 562-0477
📅
Booking & Cancellation
  • Cancel or modify your reservation up to 24 hours before pickup — no fee.
  • Each booking includes one free reschedule. A fee applies on any subsequent reschedule.
  • Longer bookings get better rates — weekly rates auto-apply at 7+ days.
⏱️
Late Returns
  • A 2-hour grace window applies to all returns — unless another rental is scheduled immediately after, in which case the grace window may not apply.
  • $30/hour late fee applies after the grace period.
  • Grace window and late fees are at operator's discretion based on scheduling. Text or call us early if you're running late — (360) 562-0477.
🔑
Pickup & Access
  • An active reservation is required. Do not show up without one — the lot is access-controlled and your code only works on your confirmed rental day.
  • Pickup is fully contactless and available 24/7.
  • Contact-assisted pickup is available with 12–24 hours advance notice.
  • A $50 fee applies for improper parking or failure to return the trailer to its designated spot.
⚠️
Damage & Liability
  • Maintenance and mechanical failures through normal use are on us.
  • Renters are financially responsible for all damage caused by negligence, overloading, tire blowouts, accidents, or misuse of equipment.
  • Pre-existing damage is documented via walk-around inspection photos at pickup — you won't be charged for something that was already there.
Note: Contact us immediately in the event of any accident or damage at (360) 562-0477.
🛡️
Insurance
  • Valid auto insurance that covers towing a trailer is required. Confirm your coverage with your provider before pickup.
  • Optional damage protection through MBA Insurance is available to add at booking:

🚫
Prohibited
  • No hazardous materials — chemicals, flammables, bio-waste, or anything requiring special disposal.
  • No illegal items of any kind.
  • No large concrete chunks or slabs in the dump trailer. Concrete bags are permitted.
  • Dump trailer must not exceed the 8.5" max fill line (~2.5 cubic yards) for dense material such as asphalt, gravel, or concrete bags.
  • Do not exceed the trailer's GVWR or payload capacity. Overloading is the renter's liability.
🚛
Delivery
  • Standard trailer delivery available within 100 miles of Vancouver, WA with advance notice and at operator's discretion. Charged at $2/mile or $50/hour during rush hour traffic, on top of the rental fee.
  • Junk Removal / Dump Run: Free delivery within 15 miles of Vancouver, WA. $2/mile each way beyond that. See full details →
🎖️
Discounts
  • 10% discount for active duty military, veterans, police, fire, and EMS personnel. Mention it at booking.
🔧
Equipment Requirements
  • All trailers require a 2 5/16" ball hitch rated for the trailer's GVWR.
  • All trailers require a 7-way RV-style plug. A 7-way adapter is required if your vehicle only has a 4-pin connector.
  • It is the renter's responsibility to confirm their vehicle's tow rating before booking.
