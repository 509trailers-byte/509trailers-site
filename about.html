The idea begins

Two Years of Vision.
One Decision to Build It.

It started in October 2023 — not with a business plan, but with frustration. Joshua needed to rent a trailer after hours. Every option required a phone call during business hours, a person on site, or a wait until the next morning. The job didn't wait. The question that followed was simple: why doesn't this exist yet?

Two years of planning, second-guessing, and coming back to the same idea. In November 2025, 509 Trailer Rentals launched in Vancouver, WA — a fully contactless, self-serve trailer rental operation built around one principle: your schedule, not ours.

No gatekeepers. No business hours. No one you have to call. Just book, get your code, hook up, and go — at 2pm or 2am.
